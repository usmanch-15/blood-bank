\chapter{System Analysis and Requirements}\label{ch:requirements}
\section{Roles}
The \textbf{donor} manages profile, consent and availability, accepts requests, records readiness, and views history/rewards. The \textbf{receiver} creates and tracks requests/SOS and confirms donations. The \textbf{administrator} uses a role-protected dashboard for users, requests, reports, misuse handling, statistics, exports, and audits.

\section{Functional Requirements}
\begin{enumerate}[label=FR\arabic*.]
\item Register/authenticate users and maintain a Firestore profile.
\item Permit valid non-rejected/non-suspended accounts without approval gating.
\item Validate profiles, availability, consent and role-specific navigation.
\item Create requests with blood group, units, urgency, deadline, hospital, contact and coordinates.
\item Filter candidates by account state, compatibility, eligibility, availability, consent, assignment and distance.
\item Support donor accept/withdraw/readiness and receiver/admin confirmation.
\item Atomically update progress and create one donation/reward outcome per donor/request.
\item Maintain recipient notifications, request events and administrative audit records.
\item Permit only administrators to execute management operations.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Non-functional Requirements}
Security requires authentication, ownership/role checks, private subcollections, server validation, and no embedded admin password. Usability requires responsive layouts, validation and loading/empty/error states. Reliability requires transactions, idempotency, and graceful network errors. Performance requires pagination and indexed pre-filtering. Privacy requires minimization and opt-in location. Maintainability requires modular services, models, widgets, rules, and tests.
\section{Constraints, Assumptions and Feasibility}
Operation depends on Firebase configuration, indexes, quotas, billing and network access. GPS/notifications need permission and coordinates may be inaccurate. Compatibility is a search aid; clinicians must screen and cross-match. Flutter/Dart, Android tooling, Node.js, Firebase CLI/emulators and LaTeX form the software environment. Managed infrastructure makes the prototype technically and economically feasible, while production feasibility requires administrator procedures, privacy/retention policy, clinical partnerships, and local legal review.
