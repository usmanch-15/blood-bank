\section{Blood Donation and Digital Coordination}
WHO guidance places suitability assessment, infection screening, storage, and clinical governance within regulated services \cite{whoBloodSafety,whoDonationFAQ}. Coordination software must help communication without posing as a clinical decision system. A systematic review reports that blood-donation apps commonly use reminders, education, appointments, location, and urgent appeals, while evidence quality and sustained engagement vary \cite{li2023mobile}. The American Red Cross app illustrates an integrated donor journey covering appointments, history, eligibility information, and rewards \cite{redCrossApp}.

\section{Gap and Contribution}
The gap is not merely displaying donor phone numbers. A practical prototype must combine identity, compatibility, eligibility, consent, distance, multi-unit state, controlled contact disclosure, duplicate-safe confirmation, notifications, and auditability. Smart Blood Bank uses callable workflows, latitude pre-filtering plus Haversine distance, and stable donation identifiers. Rewards and certificates derive from confirmed donations. This is an engineering contribution, not proof of improved clinical outcomes.
